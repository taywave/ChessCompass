FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the checklist really free?

Yes. There is no payment required to receive it.

What rating is this designed for?

The primary audience is players around 800–1400 rating, particularly players who feel stuck and don't know what to study next.

Do I need to know chess notation?

No. The checklist is designed to be practical and accessible.

Does this teach openings?

Not as its main purpose. The goal is to help you determine what you should actually be working on before spending large amounts of time on specific areas such as opening theory.

Will this increase my rating?

There are no guaranteed rating gains. The checklist is designed to give you a clearer process for identifying weaknesses and choosing training priorities.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. The checklist is free. Future Chess Compass products and coaching may be available separately.

What happens to my email?
